# Open-only route check, October 7, 2026

Work is now limited to open datasets. No credentials, restricted clinical access or new agreements will be pursued. This is an access choice, not evidence that the original prospective cognitive endpoint can be measured.

Source-grounded findings:

- https://bdsp.io/content/vbredhzkdixefl465gzg/1.0.0/ explicitly requires credentialing and a DUA. The release includes precomputed features, not raw PSG; raw access needs a separate request. Excluded under open-only scope.
- https://bdsp.io/content/v83ha57g3u22jz0eznrf/1.0.0/ explicitly requires credentialing and a DUA. Excluded under open-only scope.
- https://zenodo.org/records/18412348 is CC BY 4.0 and contains sleep-EEG feature means from AD patients. Only features significantly correlated with CSF biomarkers were retained. Not raw transition EEG, not an unbiased validation set, not a prospective decline cohort.
- https://physionet.org/content/capslpdb/1.0.0/ is open under ODC Attribution, includes 22 RBD and 16 healthy recordings, and describes staging/CAP annotations. It does not provide a prospective cognitive endpoint. Previously audited age nonoverlap remains a barrier to the proposed healthy/RBD disease comparison; RBD labels alone do not establish neurodegeneration.
- https://www.physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/ is open under ODC Attribution and supports sleep physiology/staging work. Its described healthy aging and temazepam studies do not establish paired future cognition or neurodegenerative diagnoses.

No eligible open raw-sleep-EEG/prospective-cognition cohort was verified by this check. This is not proof that none exists. Open outcome-blind engineering can continue, but a sleep-physiology study would be a different endpoint, not completion of the clinical biomarker contract. Any such pivot must be explicit and the neurodegeneration motivation labeled untested. No matching threshold or frozen failed result changed.
