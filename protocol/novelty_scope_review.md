# Preliminary novelty review, October 7, 2026

No claim of a new general matching theory or first sleep-transition analysis is warranted.

## Verified literature leads

- https://arxiv.org/html/2210.12968 , "Overlap, matching, or entropy weights: what are we weighting for?": discusses lack of positivity/common support and how trimming or weighting can target different populations/estimands. Our application is descriptive temporal matching, not this paper's causal treatment framework. Missing support and target changes are established concepts.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-08934-3 , "Discovery of key whole-brain transitions and dynamics during human wakefulness and non-REM sleep": already studies whole-brain dynamics and state transitions. Our annotation support audit is not a competing brain-dynamics discovery or network analysis.
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-11513-0 , "Slow wave synchronization and sleep state transitions": sleep transition physiology is existing research. No slow-wave mechanism has been measured here.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32304989/ , "Dynamics of sleep: Exploring critical transitions and early warning signals": proof-of-principle attractor-state work using single-channel EEG in two healthy participants. We do not introduce attractor dynamics or an early-warning biomarker.

## Defensible contribution, pending fuller review

A reproducible, outcome-blind feasibility audit of one explicitly frozen exact-context/time/nonoverlap control contract on open annotations, with recorded impossibility bounds, a full cassette census, repeated-night checks and independent-source CAP evidence. This is a narrow empirical methods boundary and reusable audit implementation, not yet a novel or publication-ready scientific contribution. Search has not established absence of prior equivalent audits. No invented priority claim, benchmark superiority, causal discovery or neurodegenerative signature is made.
