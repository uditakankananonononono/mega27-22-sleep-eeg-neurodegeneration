# Hypothesis and endpoint preregistration draft, frozen only before data inspection

Primary biological question: Do stage-specific slow-wave organization features in N3 add reproducible information about *future clinically recorded cognitive impairment* beyond age and measured sleep-disorder burden? The endpoint in PhysioNet's 2026 Challenge is ICD-coded MCI/AD/dementia pooled, not pathologically verified Alzheimer's or a direct measurement of neurodegeneration. A different source may require a separately specified clinical endpoint; do not silently harmonize labels with different definitions.

Candidate primary feature family (no effect direction asserted): N3 delta-band absolute/relative power and within-night variation, with duration and recording quality; secondary REM transition persistence and REM spectral/entropy features. Before examining any outcome labels: select exact features (maximum ten), signal/reference/filter/QC, age/site/sex/OSA and other available clinical confounds, false-discovery correction, per-person split, outcomes and missingness rules. Confirm montage/sampling/stage compatibility before combining sources. High-dimensional connectivity/coupling are exploratory only if electrodes, resolution and staging support them.

Baselines: age-only; age plus available demographic/clinical covariates; age plus sleep architecture and apnea burden; and a source-faithful published sleep-EEG comparator if comparable implementation/labels permit. The candidate must add value beyond the appropriate fair baselines, under identical splits and tuning rules. An 'independent' test source must not inform feature choice or hyperparameters. Report negative studies and pivot on evidence, not threshold-shopping.

This is a research plan, not a discovered mechanism. Confounding by age, site, OSA, medications, depression, vascular disease and diagnostic opportunity must be quantified or acknowledged where unavailable. A high AUC alone does not establish a biomarker; interpreted feature stability and external direction replication are required.

## Prior-art collision found after this draft (2026-09-26)

A SOF prospective PSG paper already analyzes stage-specific N3 and gamma and univariate/multivariate low-density EEG with future cognitive impairment. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12353395/ . Consequently, the primary N3 delta and generic cross-channel feature list above is NOT by itself a novel discovery. Before examining any project outcome, narrow a distinctive hypothesis (e.g., stage-transition-conditioned signature or robustness to cross-cohort reference/montage drift) against this published comparator. This is a post-draft correction, not a preregistered amendment yet. Do not cherry-pick novelty after inspecting labels.

## Novelty-producing repair to external critique round 01 (2026-09-26 20:19 IST)

The original critique asked for stage-specificity and a compact hypothesis rather than unrestricted feature screening. A newly implemented outcome-blind *transition-context* feature computes within-person log bandpower changes across directly adjacent N2->REM epochs and requires at least three events. This tests a transition-state question instead of another whole-night classifier feature; source code and synthetic tests are `src/dreaming22/features.py` and `tests/test_features.py`. Unknown-stage gaps are not bridged. This is an engineering novelty **angle**, not an empirically new or biologically validated biomarker. It might fail due to montage/QC/stage scarcity and must be tested after source eligibility. SOF prior-art comparison remains necessary. Before any disease-label inspection, freeze whether this is primary or exploratory and its minimum quality thresholds.

## Exploratory transition variability arm, added before cognitive outcomes

Round-2 external critique surfaced a unit-of-analysis flaw in sparse N2->REM events. A new analysis computes the within-participant robust dispersion of each *predefined* direct stage-pair spectral transition, with at least three instances, and compares to stable-stage adjacent-window controls. This is not a pooled all-stage score: mixing stage pairs would conflate their normal spectral contrasts. Primary selection, event QC and matched controls must be frozen before any outcome labels are opened. The pilot output is stage-pair descriptive only and will not be used to pick a 'winning' pair post hoc. The full novelty is conditional on independent biological/clinical validation and prior-art audit.
