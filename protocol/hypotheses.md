# Hypothesis and endpoint preregistration draft, frozen only before data inspection

Primary biological question: Do stage-specific slow-wave organization features in N3 add reproducible information about *future clinically recorded cognitive impairment* beyond age and measured sleep-disorder burden? The endpoint in PhysioNet's 2026 Challenge is ICD-coded MCI/AD/dementia pooled, not pathologically verified Alzheimer's or a direct measurement of neurodegeneration. A different source may require a separately specified clinical endpoint; do not silently harmonize labels with different definitions.

Candidate primary feature family (no effect direction asserted): N3 delta-band absolute/relative power and within-night variation, with duration and recording quality; secondary REM transition persistence and REM spectral/entropy features. Before examining any outcome labels: select exact features (maximum ten), signal/reference/filter/QC, age/site/sex/OSA and other available clinical confounds, false-discovery correction, per-person split, outcomes and missingness rules. Confirm montage/sampling/stage compatibility before combining sources. High-dimensional connectivity/coupling are exploratory only if electrodes, resolution and staging support them.

Baselines: age-only; age plus available demographic/clinical covariates; age plus sleep architecture and apnea burden; and a source-faithful published sleep-EEG comparator if comparable implementation/labels permit. The candidate must add value beyond the appropriate fair baselines, under identical splits and tuning rules. An 'independent' test source must not inform feature choice or hyperparameters. Report negative studies and pivot on evidence, not threshold-shopping.

This is a research plan, not a discovered mechanism. Confounding by age, site, OSA, medications, depression, vascular disease and diagnostic opportunity must be quantified or acknowledged where unavailable. A high AUC alone does not establish a biomarker; interpreted feature stability and external direction replication are required.
