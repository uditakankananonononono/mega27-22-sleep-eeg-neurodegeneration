# First raw-signal result: engineering feasibility, not disease evidence

A public Sleep-EDF Expanded recording was downloaded from PhysioNet, both raw EDF bytes verified against the operator's SHA256SUMS. Source: https://www.physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/ . PSG SC4001E0-PSG.edf SHA256 `2b40a18adf76af69a42d6db1f30f31d26b369f6d27ca0050ef30147ef892b131`, paired hypnogram SC4001EC-Hypnogram.edf SHA256 `a4cf67694ade1b52a0ddd06d5817fd45d2d3e8bac5302f640f3e9cfbbf12a996`. Raw files remain local and ignored from Git; download pointers only in source page and script args.

Ran `scripts/sleep_edfx_pilot.py` with 100 Hz `EEG Fpz-Cz` and manually annotated stage grid. Observed 2 directly adjacent N2->REM transitions and 29 N2->N3 transitions in one 22.08h recording; 2 transition events are **below the three-event threshold proposed in the biomarker design**, so all bandpower numbers in the JSON are method demonstration only. There are 1,997 W epochs, reflecting the long recording's wake period; it must not be mistaken for a clinical sleep metric or total-sleep percentage. The simple amplitude guard rejected 0 epochs but is not a full artifact QC protocol. A one-person numerical contrast cannot establish population direction, reproducibility or disease association. Sleep-EDF has no early neurodegeneration label. The concrete deliverable is a working, inspectable raw EDF+hypnogram-to-stage/transition feature tool and a reproducible extraction record; the user's *biomarker*, benchmark beat, discovery and paper remain open.

## Round-2 transition variability pilot (2026-09-26)

The external ChatGPT critique proposed robust dispersion of direct stage-pair spectral changes, not disease inference. Independently implemented a per-stage-pair median absolute deviation of log bandpower changes, conditional on >=3 transitions, plus a stable-stage adjacent-window control. The same verified SC4001E0 recording has N2->N3 delta MAD 0.13186 across 29 events; adjacent N2->N2 delta MAD 0.27139 across 210 pairs. N2->REM has only two events and returns null dispersion. The stable-window control is NOT matched on event count, stage duration or time of night, so this numerical difference has no biological meaning yet. Stage-specific extraction and source-derived stage labels may introduce artifacts, and a one-recording analysis cannot be a biomarker or a population finding. Future fair comparisons must match counts/time/quality and run across people, not choose a winning stage pair from this preview.
